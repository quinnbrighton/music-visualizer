# Training and evaluation recordings

The audio models in this project were trained on solo-cello performances published on YouTube. **The recordings themselves are not
redistributed here**; this page lists the original uploads so every performer and publisher is credited and the dataset can be rebuilt.
All rights belong to the performers and rights holders. Used for personal, non-commercial research.

Roles: **test** = never trained on (held-out evaluation); **val** = held out for model selection; **train** = training data.
Two recordings were saved under their titles, without the source video ID; they are listed by title only.
