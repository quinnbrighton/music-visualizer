# References

The papers this project builds on, grouped by the stage where each one was used, with what I took from it.

## Background: encoder–decoders across vision, language and sound

Cited in the README to place the codec in context.

| Paper | Why it's relevant |
|---|---|
| Hinton & Salakhutdinov 2006, [Reducing the Dimensionality of Data with Neural Networks](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1127647) | Deep autoencoders: compress through a bottleneck, then reconstruct |
| van den Oord et al. 2017, [Neural Discrete Representation Learning (VQ-VAE)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.00937) | Discrete latents from an encoder–decoder; the basis of residual VQ |
| Esser et al. 2021, [Taming Transformers for High-Resolution Image Synthesis (VQGAN)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.09841) | Adversarially trained image autoencoder producing tokens for a transformer |
| Ramesh et al. 2021, [Zero-Shot Text-to-Image Generation (DALL·E)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.12092) | Images as discrete autoencoder tokens, generated by a transformer |
| Rombach et al. 2022, [High-Resolution Image Synthesis with Latent Diffusion Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.10752) | Generation in a compressed latent instead of pixels (Stable Diffusion) |

## 1. Codec (audio ↔ 24-dim latent)

| Paper | Used for |
|---|---|
| Engel et al. 2020, [DDSP: Differentiable Digital Signal Processing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.04643) | Harmonic-plus-noise synthesis driven by pitch and loudness. My first decoder, and the basis of the harmonic-excitation decoder |
| Caillon & Esling 2021, [RAVE: A variational autoencoder for fast and high-quality neural audio synthesis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.05011) | Two-phase training: learn the representation first, then fine-tune the decoder adversarially |
| Kong et al. 2020, [HiFi-GAN](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.05646) | Multi-period discriminator and feature-matching loss |
| Lee et al. 2022, [BigVGAN](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.04658) | Discriminator and loss-weighting recipe for a universal GAN vocoder |
| Kumar et al. 2023, [High-Fidelity Audio Compression with Improved RVQGAN (DAC)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.06546) | Multi-band STFT discriminator; residual-VQ codec design |
| Blau & Michaeli 2018, [The Perception-Distortion Tradeoff](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.06077) | Why the decoder is adversarial: at a fixed rate, low sample-level error and realism trade off |
| Défossez et al. 2022, [High Fidelity Neural Audio Compression (EnCodec)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.13438) | Codec loss balancing and STFT discriminators |
| Zeghidour et al. 2021, [SoundStream](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.03312) | Residual vector quantization: the 12-stage quantizer that gives 1.8 kbit/s |
| Siuzdak 2023, [Vocos](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.00814) | Baseline vocoder (stock and cello-fine-tuned) in the codec comparisons |
| Griffin & Lim 1984, [Signal estimation from modified short-time Fourier transform](https://doi.org/10.1109/TASSP.1984.1164317) | Phase reconstruction in the v1 spectrogram baseline |
| Kim et al. 2018, [CREPE: A Convolutional Representation for Pitch Estimation](https://arxiv.org/abs/1802.06182) | Pitch tracking for the training controls and pitch-error evaluation |
| Li et al. 2023, [MERT: Acoustic Music Understanding Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.00107) | Tested as a frozen encoder; it reconstructed worse than a log-mel encoder, so I dropped it |

## 2. Shaping the latent space

| Paper | Used for |
|---|---|
| Karras et al. 2020, [Analyzing and Improving the Image Quality of StyleGAN (StyleGAN2)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.04958) | Path-length regularization: equal latent steps should produce equal changes in sound |
| Gropp, Atzmon & Lipman 2020, [Isometric Autoencoders](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.09289) | The goal of a decoder that is close to an isometry, which motivated the sound-space objective |
| Higgins et al. 2017, [β-VAE](https://openreview.net/forum?id=Sy2fzU9gl) | First regularization attempt (independent, unit-scale channels). It worked against isometry, so I replaced it |
| Kumar, Sattigeri & Balakrishnan 2018, [Variational Inference of Disentangled Latent Concepts from Unlabeled Observations (DIP-VAE)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.00848) | Decorrelation penalty in the first attempt |
| McInnes, Healy & Melville 2018, [UMAP](https://arxiv.org/abs/1802.03426) | The 2D map of the latent space and the held-out trajectory |
