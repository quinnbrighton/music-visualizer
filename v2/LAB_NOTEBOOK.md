## Research question (the framing)
"What is the minimal, interpretable description of solo cello audio, and at what timescales does it live?"
Deliverable = rate-distortion frontier (bits/s of latent vs reconstruction error on HELD-OUT recordings) for several architectures, plus an interpretability audit of what each latent group encodes.

## Prior art used (verified by search, details to be checked against the papers before citing)
- DDSP (Engel et al., ICLR 2020): harmonic+noise synth driven by f0, loudness, timbre latent; no phase problem; monophonic.
- RAVE (Caillon & Esling 2021, arXiv 2111.05011): 2-stage VAE then adversarial; SVD of latent to find the few dims that matter.
- Disentangled Sequential AE (Li & Mandt, ICML 2018) / C-DSVAE (NeurIPS 2021): static vs per-frame latents.
- Slow Feature Analysis: orders directions by temporal slowness (we measured this ourselves).
- SAEs on audio models (arXiv 2505.18186 MusicGen; 2509.24793 MERT etc.): sparse features -> pitch/amplitude/timbre.
- Music2Latent (arXiv 2408.06500): extreme compression with consistency AE.

## Metrics (uniform, audio domain, 24 kHz): metrics.logmel_dist (lm, sc), plus
- modulation table (0.5-4, 4-10, 10-43 Hz relative amplitude variance; original = 0.555, 0.091, 0.035)
- bits/s = KL nats per frame * frame rate / ln2 (latent only; f0+loudness counted separately when used)
- interpretability: R^2 of linear probes from each latent group to {loudness, f0, spectral centroid, noisiness, vibrato extent}; swap tests; SFA ordering
- LISTENING (Quinn's ears) overrides metrics: Griffin-Lim beat Vocos by ear while CLAP said the opposite. CLAP is NOT trusted as a judge.

## Known facts to build on
- Fastest-group of nested model carries ~2% of variance: recon loses ~90% of slow/mid amplitude modulation (dynamics/vibrato) -> sounds flat; GL adds ~0.025 fast flutter on top. Fixing dynamics > fixing phase.
- Global code via contrastive loss: identifies recordings (90% kNN on 6 held-out) but swapping it barely changes sound (decoder ignores it). A decoder that NEEDS it (e.g. DDSP reverb IR / EQ conditioned on it) is the planned fix.
- Near-duplicate recordings exist across train/val (profile cosine .998); load_data+train.py drop them from val. Keep checking for leakage.

- LESSONS:
  1. Measure baselines properly. A predictor trained on the FROZEN old codec latent gives 10.3% unpredictable (12.6% after 250 steps, 10.3% after 6000). The "25% -> 11%" story was the jointly-trained predictor learning, not the latent improving; reg2 at s3000 was no better than the original codec.
  2. Per-channel unit scale works against the sound-space goal: it stretches quiet-but-important channels and amplifies quiet noisy ones (less predictable). Unit-variance/decorrelation is the disentanglement goal (beta-VAE, DIP-VAE); equal-step-equal-sound is the isometry goal (StyleGAN2 path length, isometric AEs). They agree only if each channel's spread matches its perceptual weight - ours don't, so corr/scale stalled at ~0.56 / 3x.
  3. Predictability must be scored in the same units as the sound metric (rate-distortion: bits follow variance relative to perceptual distortion), not after per-channel standardization, or inaudible noise counts as much as pitch.
  4. Correlation is not wasted bits for a vector quantizer (RVQ exploits it); predictability only lowers real bit rate with a predictive quantizer or a context entropy model.
  5. The GAN regenerates texture (bow noise, rosin) from a smooth latent; it cannot invent event timing (bow changes, accents), which must stay in the latent.
